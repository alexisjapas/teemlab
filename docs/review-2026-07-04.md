## A. Roadmap review — remaining work & what to integrate next

### Where the project stands
P0–P5 done; nutrients T2 + T3 links 1–2 done; the **cognitive substrate is now complete**
(proprioception + deliberate/costed eating). The near-term product goal (binding:
`docs/persistent-ecosystems.md`) is **rich, non-collapsing ecosystems**, with a downstream
**science of collapse factors**.

### Open threads (from ROADMAP §0/§9), grouped
- **Component emission** (agent → environment; the symmetric of absorption). One
  per-component-layer mechanism unifies **corpses/turnover, organic waste, toxicity,
  pheromones**. Double-edged for persistence: turnover *stabilises* (a flat carrying capacity),
  toxin accumulation *destabilises* (a new endogenous collapse mode). *Open sub-question:* the
  audio/wave modality does not fit the diffusion-layer model.
- **Nutrients T3 finish**: per-species absorption; multiple nutrients (makes the 50/50 heatmap
  budget real); the **conservation-at-reproduction invariant** (today the nutrient paid per
  child is *destroyed* — an explicit interim Law-9 debt); **emergent targeting** (Law 8 — eat
  what holds the needed nutrient, replacing the explicit relation table).
- **Restraint actually selected** — the *payoff* of the substrate just built: a scenario where
  spatial viscosity (limited dispersal) makes restraint pay. This is a research scenario, not a
  mechanism; it validates the whole substrate thesis.
- **Collapse-factor science instrument**: a collapse metric distinguishing *irreversible*
  collapse from a *reversible* trough, single-factor gradients, running past the first trough.
  Extends the existing `sweep` bin.
- **P5 polish**: live match spectator, Pause/Step-generation; weight crossover / NEAT (item 21).
- **Turnover / density mortality** (`crush`) — deferred pending a *portable* formulation
  (overlap count/depth, not raw contact impulse). Now largely **subsumed by emission** (corpses
  = biomass emitted at death).
- **Long tail**: editor library management + catalog metadata; toroidal arena (blocked on
  Avian); harden the two constitutions (governance).

### Recommendation — integrate next, in order
1. **Component emission via per-component layers** (highest leverage). It is the single missing
   mechanism behind four wishlist items and both poles of the persistence story (turnover +
   toxicity). The `NutrientField` already *is* a per-component layer with conservative
   `add`/`take`/`diffuse` — emission is the *write* direction (agent → field), the mirror of
   the existing `absorb`. Start with **corpses/turnover** (a dying body deposits biomass into a
   "carrion" layer) — it delivers the flat carrying capacity the roadmap keeps flagging as
   missing, and recycling already proves the death→field deposit path.
2. **Restraint-under-spatial-viscosity demonstration** (cheap, high narrative value). The
   substrate is built and unproven; a scenario tying `seed_dispersal` (locality) to a shared,
   depletable resource would show whether restraint is *selected*. Low code cost (mostly a
   scenario + a driver), and it closes the loop on the work just shipped.
3. **Conservation-at-reproduction invariant** (correctness debt). Pairs naturally with emission
   — the nutrient paid for a child should be *transformed/carried*, not annihilated (Law 9).
4. **Collapse-factor instrument** (the downstream product goal) — once persistent ecosystems
   are routinely reachable.

Sequencing note: 1 and 3 reinforce each other (emission closes the matter loop; the
reproduction invariant is the last leak). 2 is independent and can slot in anytime as a
validation milestone.

## B. Project review — bugs, inefficiencies, code quality

### Overall
The codebase is in strong shape: clean `clippy`/`fmt`, **no `unsafe`, no `TODO/FIXME/HACK`** in
source, panics/`unwrap` confined to tests and startup/bin paths, RON loading is `Result`-based
and the windowed path handles load errors gracefully (`runs.rs:515`). Documentation is
unusually good (the *why*, laws cited) and testing is falsifiable and multi-seed with
chaos-tripwires. The findings below are refinements, not alarms.

### Findings

**B1 — Known crash: `record --select off --no-hud` (low).** `dataviz::draw_viz`
(`dataviz.rs:380`) takes `selection: Res<Selection>`, but `Selection` is only inserted when
`--select != off` → "resource does not exist" panic for that flag combo. *Fix:*
`Option<Res<Selection>>`, or gate `draw_viz` on the HUD being enabled. Already logged in ROADMAP
§9; still live.

**B2 — MLP-tripwire fragility (medium, inherent).** `tests/mlp` asserts a ≥1.5× learning gap
across 5 seeds against a **committed, regenerated** capture (`07`/`09`), produced by a training
run that is not bit-deterministic under the parallel scheduler (Law 10) and is high-variance on
living food (§7). During this change one seed was marginal (0.50 → 0.61) before re-picking the
training seed (→ 77, gen-9). Risk: a future `cargo run --bin train` regeneration could drift and
re-fail a marginal seed. The best-over-run capture I added reduces this, but the coupling of a
CI tripwire to a stochastic artifact remains a latent flake source. *Options:* capture-select by
a **foraging score** rather than generation; or widen the seed set / lower the margin claim to
what the mechanism robustly delivers.

**B3 — `MlpBrain::think` per-tick allocations (perf; documented, deferred).** `think` allocates
`layers + 1` `Vec<f32>` per MLP agent per tick (`input_vector` + each `Layer::forward` collect,
`brain.rs:~368/573`). Negligible in the continuous regime, **significant under P5's large
generational batches**. Already analysed in ROADMAP §9 (a scratch buffer would break the
`state = topology + weights` invariant, or blur the `Perception → Action` contract). *Suggestion:*
handle profiler-in-hand when a batch actually needs it — a `thread_local` scratch reused in
`decide`, validated by measurement.

**B4 — `NutrientField::diffuse` is full-grid every tick (perf).** O(res²) per tick regardless of
where nutrient actually is (`res = 256` → 65k cells/tick, 64×/s). This was the bulk of the
measured −10% from the Bevy 0.19 bump (nutrients −10.1%). Correct and mass-conserving, but
wasteful when nutrient is concentrated near a few sources. *Options (all optional):* diffuse
every N ticks; a coarser field; or skip rows/regions known empty. Profile first (§6 says confirm
raycast vs field is the real cost).

**B5 — Parallel-structure duplication when adding a gene (maintainability).** A new gene touches
~8 hand-maintained sites: `Genotype` field + its `Default`, the `TRAITS` entry, `Mutability`
(struct + `all_fixed` + `Default`), and `SimConfig` (`*_bounds` field + its `Default`). The
`TRAITS` table centralises the mutation/editor wiring and a test guards `bounds`/`bounds_mut`
consistency — but **nothing guarantees every `Genotype` gene has a `TRAITS` entry or a
`Mutability` field**. A gene added to `Genotype` but omitted from `TRAITS` compiles and silently
never mutates / never appears in the editor; omitted from `Mutability` it silently can't be
frozen. *Suggestion (optional):* a test asserting `TRAITS.len()` equals the gene count (or a
field-enumeration check), so the parallel lists can't silently drift. (This change added
`act_cost` to all sites and is covered by the existing suite, but the guard would prevent a
future miss.)

**B6 — `interact` rebuilds relation colliders each tick (micro).** `reaches: Vec<Collider>` is
rebuilt every tick (`interaction.rs:~118`). It is per-*relation* (few), not per-actor, and the
code comments already note building parry colliders isn't free. Could be cached across ticks
while `relations` is unchanged. Very low priority.

### Non-issues (deliberate, verified)
- `#[allow(clippy::too_many_arguments)]` — all on ECS systems / CLI drivers (the Bevy idiom).
- `act_cost` charged on *intent held* even with no target in range — an intentional *effort*
  model (documented in `ecology.rs` / the gene doc), the lever that makes restraint pay.
- Hand-written brains holding `act = 1.0` (Sessile included) — required for byte-identical flora
  self-competition; verified by the green chaos drivers.

## Bottom line
Ship-quality change; strong codebase. The single most valuable next integration is
**component emission (per-component layers), starting with corpses/turnover** — it unlocks the
flat carrying capacity, the toxicity destabiliser, and reuses the existing field abstraction.
The only latent CI fragility worth watching is **B2** (the MLP tripwire coupled to a stochastic
regenerated capture); the rest are documented, low-severity refinements.
